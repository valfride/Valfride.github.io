About

Research grounded in real operating conditions.

My work investigates how visual recognition systems can remain useful when images are small, blurred, noisy, compressed, or captured at long distances. I design super-resolution models, task-aware losses, benchmarks, and datasets that connect image restoration with downstream recognition performance.

I am particularly interested in reproducible research for automatic license plate recognition and intelligent transportation systems, including public datasets, transparent evaluation protocols, and open-source implementations.
